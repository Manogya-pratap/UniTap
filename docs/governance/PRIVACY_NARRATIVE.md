# 5️⃣ PUBLIC PRIVACY NARRATIVE PACK

*(Trust > Technology)*

## 🎯 Purpose
Prevent fear, misinformation, and backlash.

---

## 🔍 What We Clearly Say

### We DO:
* Track attendance
* Process transactions
* Store audit logs

### We DO NOT:
* Track location continuously
* Store card numbers
* Sell personal data
* Monitor behavior outside campus

---

## 📢 Sample Public Statement
> “UniTap is designed so that identity lives in secure systems — not on plastic cards or public devices.”

---

## 👪 Parent-Focused Message
> “Your child’s spending and attendance are visible only with consent and controlled access.”
