# 4️⃣ REGULATORY PHASING STRATEGY

*(How you stay legal while growing)*

## 🎯 Purpose
Avoid **premature regulation** while staying compliant.

---

## 🧩 Phase-wise Strategy

### Phase 1 – Closed Campus (Low Risk)
* UPI-based wallet
* No stored value
* No KYC mandatory
* College-only usage
👉 Minimal RBI exposure

### Phase 2 – Semi-Closed Wallet (Campus & Local Merchants)
* Bank PPI partner
* Low limits
* Basic KYC
* Alumni enablement
* Expansion to local merchants near campus
* **Kong Gateway Security**: Begin mTLS/HMAC integration with Bank partners.

### Phase 3 – Open Wallet / Transit (Use Everywhere)
* Full KYC
* Metro/Transit integration
* National rollout
* **Mobile App QR Code payment enabled for off-campus merchants**
* **Bank-Grade Infrastructure**: Full Kong Konnect implementation for real-time bank approvals and settlements.

---
